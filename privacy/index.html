Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Glowtrack is built on one simple principle: your skin stays on your phone. Skin analysis runs entirely on your device — your photos are never uploaded to any server, ours or anyone else's.

This policy explains what information Glowtrack handles, where it lives, what little of it ever leaves your device, and the choices you have. It applies to the Glowtrack iOS app ("Glowtrack", "we", "us"). Because the app analyses a photo of your face, section 2 is devoted entirely to face data — what is collected, every use of it, sharing, retention, and how to withdraw consent and delete it.

1. Who we are

Glowtrack is developed and operated by the Glowtrack team. For any privacy question or request, contact us at vistalabs.dev@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy under applicable data-protection laws, including the EU/UK GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK (Law No. 6698).

2. Face data

Glowtrack's skin analysis looks at a photo of your face, so this section sets out in one place exactly what face data the app collects, every use we make of it, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you withdraw your consent and delete it. The short version: face data never leaves your device. Every step below happens on your iPhone or iPad. No selfie, no facial landmark and no measurement derived from them is transmitted to us or to anyone else, at any time, for any purpose.

2.1 What face data we collect

  • A selfie. The photo you take in the app, or pick from your photo library, after you choose to start a scan. Nothing is captured in the background: the camera is live only while you are on the scan screen, and only if you granted camera access.
  • Facial landmarks — in memory, for one analysis only. While the scan is being analysed, Apple's on-device Vision framework locates the face in the photo and the outlines of facial regions (forehead, cheeks, nose, chin, and the brow, eye and lip areas, which are excluded from measurement) so that readings are taken from skin rather than from hair, eyes or background. These landmark coordinates exist only in the device's working memory for the few seconds the analysis takes and are discarded when it ends. They are never written to storage and never transmitted.
  • Skin measurements derived from the photo. Inside those regions the app computes non-identifying image statistics — brightness, redness, tone and texture variance, shine — and converts them into your skin scores (overall score, estimated skin-appearance range, hydration, texture, tone evenness, and a per-region summary).
  • What is actually saved. Only the selfie itself and those numeric scores, and only to the app's private database on your own device, so that you can look back at a scan and compare your progress.
No faceprint, and no facial recognition. Glowtrack does not create, derive, store or use a faceprint, face template, face embedding, face signature or any other biometric identifier. It performs no facial recognition, identity verification or identity matching; it cannot recognise you, tell two people apart, or match a face against any database. It makes no emotion, demographic or age determinations, and it does not use ARKit, the TrueDepth camera, depth maps or Face ID data. The landmarks described above are geometric outlines used to place measurement zones on a single photo, and they are destroyed as soon as that analysis finishes.

2.2 How we use face data — the complete list

  • To measure the visible condition of your skin in the photo you just provided, and turn it into that scan's result.
  • To show you that result, your scan history and your before/after comparisons inside the app, on your device.
  • To choose which routine steps, tips and product suggestions the app shows you, on your device.

Those are all of the uses, and there are no disclosures. Face data is never used for advertising, marketing, analytics, profiling, identity verification, security, law enforcement, research, or to train, fine-tune or improve any machine-learning model — ours or anyone else's. It is never combined with data from other sources, and it is never used to build a profile of you outside the app.

2.3 Sharing face data

We do not share face data with any third party. Your selfies, your facial landmarks and your scan images are never sold, rented, licensed, disclosed or otherwise made available to any third party — no service provider, advertiser, analytics provider, partner or affiliate — and they are never uploaded to any server, including our own. They are not included in any network request the app makes. In particular, the Premium AI coach transmits text only: section 4 lists every field that leaves your device for any feature, and no image, landmark or face measurement appears in that list.

Because no third party ever receives face data, no third party processes, stores or retains it. Should this ever change, we would obtain your explicit consent first, update this policy before the change took effect, and require by written contract that any such third party provide protection for the face data that is the same as or equal to the protection stated in this policy — including the limits on use, the prohibitions on identification, advertising and model training, and the deletion rights set out here. We have no plans to do this.

2.4 How long face data is retained

  • On our servers: never received, therefore never retained. There is no server-side copy of your face data to keep, back up, log or archive — not for a day, not for a second.
  • On your device: a selfie and its scores remain in the app's local database until you delete them in one of the ways below. Nothing expires on its own, because the history exists purely so that you can see your own progress; you decide how much of it to keep.
  • Facial landmarks: retained in memory only for the duration of a single analysis, then discarded. They are never persisted.

2.5 Withdrawing consent and deleting your face data

You consent to this processing by choosing to take or select a photo and start a scan — the app never scans on its own initiative. You can withdraw that consent at any time, with no effect on the rest of the app: scanning is optional, and your routine, logs, products and reminders all work without it.

  • Stop any further collection. Simply stop scanning, or revoke the app's access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and → Photos (or Settings → Glowtrack). With access revoked, Glowtrack cannot obtain a photo of your face at all.
  • Delete all of it, inside the app. Open your profile from the avatar on the Today screen and tap "Delete all my data" ("Tüm verilerimi sil") under Data, then confirm. This immediately and permanently erases every selfie and every scan result from the device, together with your profile, routine history, daily logs, experiments and coach conversation. It cannot be undone.
  • Delete a guest's scans. Scans taken by someone else in Guest mode are listed under Profile → Guest scans and can be deleted individually by swiping.
  • Delete the app. Removing Glowtrack from your device deletes its entire container, including every selfie, scan and derived score.

Because we hold no copy of your face data, deleting it on your device is complete deletion: there is no request to send us, no account to close, and no server copy that could outlive it. If you would like written confirmation of this, or have any question about face data, email vistalabs.dev@gmail.com and we will respond within the timelines required by law.

3. Information that stays on your device

Everything listed below is stored only in the app's local database on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to third parties.

Selfies and skin analyses Skin analysis is performed on your device using Apple's on-device image processing (the Vision framework). Your selfies, analysis results (skin score, estimated skin-appearance range, hydration, texture and tone metrics) and your scan history are stored locally. No photo ever leaves your phone — not for analysis, not for backups, not for the AI coach. Section 2 covers face data in full.
Profile and routine Your onboarding answers (skin type, concerns, goals, sensitivities, habits, budget and similar preferences), your skincare routine, custom steps and product choices are stored on your device.
Daily logs and check-ins Routine completions, streaks, mood, water and sleep check-ins are stored locally and used only to show your own progress inside the app.
Cycle information If you enable cycle tracking, Glowtrack keeps exactly two data points: the start date of your last period and your average cycle length. We treat this as sensitive health information: it is stored only on your device, is never sent to any server, and is used solely to time gentle, cycle-aware skincare suggestions and optional local reminders. You can turn cycle tracking off at any time, and general trends shown around it are not medical advice.
Guest scans If someone else scans their face on your device as a "Guest", that scan is stored only on your device, is kept strictly separate from your own progress and comparisons, and can be deleted individually inside the app.
Coach conversations Your chat history with the coach is stored on your device so the conversation can continue where you left off. Note that the Premium AI coach also sends each message onward to generate a reply — see section 4.
Product label photos When you photograph an ingredient list, the photo is read on your device using Apple's Vision framework. The photo is not saved to our systems and is never uploaded — only you see it. Ingredient scoring also runs entirely on your device against a dictionary shipped inside the app.
Reminders Routine and cycle reminders are scheduled locally through iOS notifications. No notification content passes through our servers — we do not have any.

Premium subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details; those are governed by Apple's privacy policy. Subscription state (active / expired / trial) is validated through Adapty as described above.

  • No profiling of your behavior across apps or websites.
  • No faceprints, face templates or biometric identifiers, no facial recognition and no identity matching — see section 2.
  • No uploading of your selfies, scan images or scan history — these never leave your device, for any feature. 7. Data retention and deletion

Face data has its own retention and deletion rules, set out in sections 2.4 and 2.5: we retain none of it, ever, because it never reaches us, and you can erase the copy on your device at any time.

The only record we hold on a server is the coach message counter described in section 4: a random install identifier, a date and a number. It contains no message content and is deleted automatically after 30 days. To have it removed sooner, email us — the identifier is shown nowhere, so include the approximate dates you used the coach and we will locate and delete it.

Catalog contributions are the second kind of server record. The text part (name, brand, routine step, ingredient list, barcode, install identifier) is kept so we can add the product to the catalog and trace what was added. A front-of-pack photo, if you chose to send one, is deleted as soon as the contribution is processed and in any case within 90 days. To have a contribution removed, email us with the product name and the approximate date.

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in direct control of it:

  • Deleting the app permanently deletes all your data — selfies, analyses, profile, routines, logs, cycle information and chat history. There is no server copy to remove.
  • Guest scans can be deleted individually inside the app, and your profile details can be edited at any time.
  • Data included in your device backups (iCloud or local) is governed by Apple's backup encryption and your own backup settings.

8. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private container, protected by iOS sandboxing and your device's encryption. Network calls the app does make (listed in section 4) use HTTPS. We hold no copy of your photos, scans, profile or logs anywhere, so the most effective protection — keeping your data on hardware you control — remains the default. The one server-side record we do keep (the coach message counter) sits behind row-level security and is reachable only by our own function, never by the app.
