5. About the analysis

Skin analysis is performed on your device from a single photograph. Results depend heavily on lighting, camera quality, angle, distance, make-up and image processing applied by your phone. Scores are relative indicators intended to show change over time under similar conditions; they are estimates, not measurements, and two photos taken minutes apart may differ.

Ingredient analysis reads the ingredient list you photograph, paste or fetch by barcode, and scores it against a dictionary shipped inside the App. Text recognition can misread small print, and ingredients that are not in our dictionary are excluded from the score rather than judged — the App shows you how many were recognised. Concentrations cannot be known from an ingredient list, so the score is an informed indication, not a laboratory assessment.

We make no promise that using the App will improve your skin or produce any particular result.
